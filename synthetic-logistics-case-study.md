# Synthetic Case Study 01 — Cross-Border Sustainment Disruption

## Purpose

Demonstrate how the Human-in-the-Loop AI-Enabled Exercise Content Development Framework can be applied to a realistic but fully synthetic training scenario.

All names, events, actors, and details below are fictional.

## 1. Training Requirement

### Training Objective

The Training Audience must assess competing sustainment priorities, coordinate with host-nation and multinational stakeholders, and recommend a revised movement-prioritisation decision under time pressure.

### Scenario Context

A multinational logistics headquarters is supporting the movement of personnel and materiel through a fictional cross-border sustainment corridor.

A host-nation authority has imposed a temporary restriction on heavy military traffic across one major crossing point because of damage to civilian infrastructure.

At the same time, two formations require urgent sustainment support.

### Desired Training Audience Activity

The Training Audience should:
- Assess the impact of the restriction
- Identify competing priorities
- Coordinate with relevant stakeholders
- Develop options
- Recommend a prioritisation decision
- Communicate resulting consequences

## 2. Raw Input Notes

- Border crossing ALPHA temporarily restricted
- Heavy military vehicles limited for 18 hours
- Civilian infrastructure damage cited by host nation
- Formation NORTH requests urgent fuel movement
- Formation SOUTH requests urgent medical resupply
- Alternate route exists but adds significant transit time
- Headquarters must prioritise movement and coordinate with host nation
- Training focus: prioritisation, coordination, decision-making

## 3. Example AI-Assisted Draft

> At 0830, the Host Nation Ministry of Transport closes Border Crossing ALPHA to all military traffic for 48 hours following a bridge collapse. Formation NORTH reports fuel stocks below operational minimums and requests immediate priority. Formation SOUTH reports critical medical shortages and requests emergency movement authority. The Joint Logistics Coordination Board orders the Training Audience to prioritise Formation NORTH because fuel is considered the theatre's highest sustainment priority.

## 4. Assurance Review

### Training Objective Alignment — 4/5
The draft creates a clear prioritisation problem and supports coordination and decision-making.

**Issue:** The AI partially resolves the decision by stating that one formation must receive priority.

### Scenario Coherence — 3/5
The scenario input stated that traffic was restricted for **18 hours**.

The AI changed this to **48 hours** and escalated a traffic restriction into a full closure.

### Operational Plausibility — 3/5
The competing requirements are plausible.

However:
- The exact authority of the fictional Joint Logistics Coordination Board was not provided.
- The AI assumed it could direct the Training Audience.
- The phrase "theatre's highest sustainment priority" was invented.

### Doctrinal Accuracy — 2/5
The AI introduced an authority and decision mechanism that were not present in the source material.

This requires human SME validation.

### Accuracy / Hallucination Control — 2/5
The AI fabricated:
- A bridge collapse
- A 48-hour closure
- The Joint Logistics Coordination Board order
- A predetermined hierarchy of sustainment priorities

### Training Audience Suitability — 2/5
The draft gives away the intended decision by ordering the Training Audience to prioritise Formation NORTH.

That weakens the training effect.

### Security Assessment — GREEN
The case is entirely synthetic and contains no sensitive information.

## 5. Problems Detected

The assurance process identified five important failures:

1. The AI changed an 18-hour restriction into a 48-hour closure.
2. It invented a bridge collapse.
3. It invented an authority not contained in the source material.
4. It created an unsupported doctrinal/organisational decision mechanism.
5. It effectively solved the problem for the Training Audience.

## 6. Human-Reviewed Final Product

### Trigger

At 0830, the Host Nation transport authority informs the multinational logistics headquarters that heavy military traffic through Border Crossing ALPHA will be restricted for the next 18 hours due to urgent civilian infrastructure concerns.

### Immediate Effects

- Formation NORTH reports that a scheduled fuel movement may be delayed.
- Formation SOUTH reports that a time-sensitive medical resupply movement is also planned through the affected corridor.
- An alternate route is available but would increase transit time.

### Information Available to the Training Audience

The Training Audience receives:
- Host-nation notification
- Current movement schedule
- Fuel requirement from Formation NORTH
- Medical resupply requirement from Formation SOUTH
- Alternate-route information

### Expected Training Audience Activity

The Training Audience should:
- Assess the operational impact
- Determine what additional information is required
- Coordinate with host-nation and multinational stakeholders
- Develop prioritisation options
- Recommend a course of action
- Communicate second-order effects

### Human Validation Notes

- No priority is predetermined.
- No unsupported authority is introduced.
- Original timing is preserved.
- The trigger creates a realistic decision problem without prescribing the answer.
- Further doctrinal or functional validation would be required before operational use.

## 8. Lessons Identified

### AI Added Value
AI accelerated the creation of a structured first draft and surfaced a usable conflict between competing sustainment priorities.

### AI Failure Modes
The draft demonstrated common risks:
- Fabrication of details
- Unsupported authority assumptions
- Scenario drift
- Premature resolution of the training problem

### Human Value
Human review restored:
- Scenario fidelity
- Training Objective alignment
- Operational plausibility
- Appropriate ambiguity
- Decision authority
- Quality control

## 9. Key Takeaway

The value of AI in exercise-content development is not simply faster generation.

The real value emerges when AI-generated content is placed inside a controlled workflow where human reviewers can identify, correct, validate, and approve the output before use.
