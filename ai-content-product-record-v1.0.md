# AI Content Product Record v1.0

## Purpose

This record provides traceability for AI-assisted exercise-content products.

It captures the relationship between:

- Source material
- AI tool
- Prompt version
- Human reviewer
- SME validation
- Corrections
- Assurance status
- Final approval

The record is intended to support responsible AI use, auditability, version control, and human accountability.
